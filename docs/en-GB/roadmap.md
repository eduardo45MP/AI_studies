# 🗺️ Roadmap — AI_Studies

This roadmap describes the **intentional evolution** of the **AI_Studies** repository.

It is not a rigid task list nor a fixed schedule. Its role is to guide **cognitive priorities**, ensuring that the repository evolves as a **coherent learning system**, not as an unstructured accumulation of experiments.

---

## Roadmap Principles

Before the phases, a few guiding principles:

* **Learning before volume**
  Fewer well-documented studies are preferable to many shallow ones.

* **Incremental evolution**
  Each phase must generate independent cognitive value.

* **Conscious exploration**
  New topics are introduced by intellectual necessity, not by trends.

* **Natural graduation**
  Mature ideas should leave AI_Studies and become independent projects.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundations and Cognitive Base

**Objective:** consolidate solid fundamentals and establish disciplined, structured study habits.

Main focuses:

* Practical review of Machine Learning fundamentals
* Manual implementation of classic algorithms (when feasible)
* Deep understanding of:

  * bias and variance
  * overfitting and underfitting
  * validation strategies and metrics
* Initial organisation of notebooks and datasets

Expected outcomes:

* Clear, well-commented notebooks
* Simple but well-understood experiments
* Consistent technical vocabulary

---

## Phase 2 — Statistical and Predictive Models

**Objective:** develop intuition for predictive modelling and time series.

Main focuses:

* Linear and non-linear regression
* Tree-based models
* Time series (ARIMA, SARIMA, Prophet, basic LSTMs)
* Feature engineering for temporal data
* Model evaluation under realistic conditions

Expected outcomes:

* Comparative studies between approaches
* Clear understanding of limitations and biases
* Synthesis articles in `articles/`

---

## Phase 3 — Deep Learning and Representations

**Objective:** understand neural networks as representation systems, not just tools.

Main focuses:

* Dense neural networks
* CNNs for computer vision
* RNNs, LSTMs, and Transformers (conceptual + practical)
* Training dynamics, regularisation, and stability
* Failure analysis and model behaviour

Expected outcomes:

* Trained models with explicit trade-off awareness
* Experiments focused on representation and generalisation
* Clear documentation of architectural decisions

---

## Phase 4 — Natural Language Processing

**Objective:** explore language as a structural and semantic form of data.

Main focuses:

* Text preprocessing and representation
* Classical models (TF-IDF, embeddings)
* Transformers and pre-trained models
* Controlled fine-tuning
* Qualitative and quantitative evaluation

Expected outcomes:

* Reproducible NLP experiments
* Critical analysis of results
* Conceptual synthesis in natural language

---

## Phase 5 — Generative Models

**Objective:** understand generation as a probabilistic and control problem.

Main focuses:

* Autoencoders and VAEs
* Autoregressive models
* Diffusion models (conceptual and experimental level)
* Limits, risks, and hallucinations
* Generative quality evaluation

Expected outcomes:

* Well-documented exploratory experiments
* Clear understanding of practical limitations
* Critical reflections recorded

---

## Phase 6 — Evaluation, Ethics, and Limits

**Objective:** develop critical judgement about responsible AI usage.

Main focuses:

* Metrics beyond accuracy
* Bias and fairness analysis
* Robustness and generalisation
* Systemic failures and risks
* Technical and societal limits of AI

Expected outcomes:

* Critical, well-founded studies
* Reflective articles in `articles/`
* Consolidated decision criteria

---

## Continuous Phase — Synthesis and Consolidation

**Objective:** transform experimentation into durable knowledge.

Recurring activities:

* Review and refactor older notebooks
* Write synthesis articles
* Remove or archive obsolete studies
* Selective integration with the portfolio

This phase never ends.

---

## Roadmap Success Criteria

The roadmap is successful if, over time, the repository:

* Becomes **clearer**, not more complex
* Reflects genuine growth in understanding
* Allows easy revisiting of past studies
* Serves as a solid foundation for future decisions

---

## Final Note

This roadmap is a **directional instrument**, not a source of pressure.

It exists to protect AI_Studies from two common risks:

* exploration without learning
* accumulation without understanding

Learning well takes time.
This repository was created to respect that.
